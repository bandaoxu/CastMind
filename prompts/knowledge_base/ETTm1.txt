## ETTm1 Dataset Briefing

ETTm1 is the 15-minute electricity transformer temperature series.
The endogenous target is oil temperature (OT). HUFL/HULL/MUFL/MULL/LUFL/LULL are load features.
Daily and weekly cycles remain strong; load increases often lift OT with a short lag.
