## Windy Power Dataset Briefing

15-minute wind farm generation with meteorological drivers:
direct radiation, 80 m wind speed/direction, 2 m temperature, 2 m humidity, and precipitation.
Power is dominated by 80 m wind speed; cut-in/cut-out behavior makes the series nonlinear and noisy.
