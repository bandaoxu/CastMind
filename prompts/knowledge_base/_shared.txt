# Shared Knowledge Base (K) — conceptual / domain knowledge for AlphaCast-style forecasting

This knowledge base supplies abstract forecasting concepts used by the Generator
together with dataset-specific empirical notes (when present).

## Conceptual knowledge
- Time-series forecasting predicts future values of an endogenous target from a
  look-back window, optionally using exogenous covariates.
- Useful inductive biases include seasonality (e.g., daily cycles for hourly data),
  trend, autocorrelation, and regime changes after shocks.
- Auxiliary statistical / deep / foundation models provide weak supervisory
  forecasts and case-library references; they are not the final answer.
- Multi-agent co-reasoning: Investigator selects evidence (including F_selected);
  Generator produces a raw forecast and chain-of-thought; Reflector audits
  reliability and may request another round.

## Empirical-pattern guidance (generic)
- Prefer models that historically win on similar look-back shapes in the case library.
- When seasonal strength is high, seasonal baselines and seasonal-capable models
  are often more trustworthy than purely trend-based extrapolations.
- When volatility is high, avoid over-confident point adjustments far from the
  auxiliary reference prediction without supporting exogenous evidence.
