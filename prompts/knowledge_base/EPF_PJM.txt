## EPF_PJM Dataset Briefing

PJM COMED zonal day-ahead prices (hourly). The target is Price.
Exogenous drivers are the system load forecast and the COMED zonal load forecast.
Load-driven peaks are common; treat large evening spikes as load events rather than a new trend.
