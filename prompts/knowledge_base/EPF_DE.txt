## EPF_DE Dataset Briefing

German day-ahead electricity prices (hourly). The target is Price.
Exogenous drivers are the aggregated wind/solar generation forecast and the Amprion zonal load forecast.
High renewable output can push prices down or negative; load still dominates weekday peaks.
