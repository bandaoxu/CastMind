## ETTh1 Dataset Briefing

ETTh1 records hourly measurements from an electricity transformer.
The endogenous target is oil temperature (OT). The remaining columns
(HUFL, HULL, MUFL, MULL, LUFL, LULL) are load features that often
move with OT and can serve as exogenous context.

Typical patterns:
- Strong daily seasonality (24 hours) and weekly periodicity.
- OT tends to rise with higher load and during hotter seasons.
- Sudden spikes may follow load shocks rather than a simple trend.
