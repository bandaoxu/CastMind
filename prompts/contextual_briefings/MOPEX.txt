## MOPEX Dataset Briefing

Daily catchment hydrology. The endogenous target is streamflow.
Train/val/test lengths follow AlphaCast Table 6: (8544, 1344, 2544).
Exogenous drivers are mean areal precipitation (MAP), climatic potential evaporation (CPE),
daily maximum temperature (Tmax), and daily minimum temperature (Tmin).
Flow responds to rainfall with catchment lag; hot dry spells increase evapotranspiration and lower baseflow.
